game's data and memory do, and wrote it down for everyone. Thank you.

## Sources and credits

The pages that the help pages take their facts from:

- The EUD Book (<https://armoha.github.io/eud-book/>), and its offset table
  (<https://armoha.github.io/eud-book/offset_table.html>): the game's memory and the fields that a map can change.
- EUD Editor 2 (<https://github.com/Buizz/EUDEditor>, MIT license, LeeJungHun): the .dat fields, their names and their
  places in the game's memory.
- The StarCraft DAT page of Darkened Fantasies (<https://www.darkenedfantasies.com/resources/?i=sc_fmt_dat>): what each
  field of the .dat files does in the game, its flags and its values.
- EUD Editor 3 (<https://github.com/Buizz/EUD-Editor-3>): the EUD logic of its triggers.
- staredit.net (<https://staredit.net/>): the community's forum and wiki.

Each page names its own sources at its end.
